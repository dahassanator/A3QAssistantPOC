# Ala. Code § 36-25-6 - Use of Contributions

Contributions to an office holder, a candidate, or to a public official's inaugural or transitional fund shall not be converted to personal use.

History: Acts 1973, No. 1056, p. 1699, §4; Acts 1975, No. 130, p. 603, §1; Acts 1995, No. 95-194, p. 269, §1; Acts 1997, No. 97-651, p. 1217, §1.
