# Ala. Code § 36-25-30 - Construction of Chapter

This chapter shall be construed in pari materia with other laws dealing with the subject of ethics.

History: Acts 1973, No. 1056, p. 1699, §32; Acts 1975, No. 130, p. 603, §1; Acts 1995, No. 95-194, p. 269, §1.
