# Who Must File a Statement of Economic Interests (current)

Statutory authority: Ala. Code §§ 36-25-14 and 36-25-15.

## Salary threshold

Appointed public officials and public employees at the state, county or municipal level must file if they earn $106,000 or more annually. That threshold was adjusted on December 3, 2025 and took effect January 1, 2026.

This figure changes periodically. Older Ethics Commission documents state $50,000 or $75,000; those are superseded.

## Categories required to file

- All elected public officials at the state, county, or municipal level of government or their instrumentalities
- Appointed public officials and public employees at the state, county or municipal level earning $106,000 or more annually
- Candidates, filing on the date the candidate files his or her qualifying papers; for independent candidates, when complying with the requirements of Section 17-9-3
- Members of the Alabama Ethics Commission
- Appointed members of statewide boards and commissions, excluding purely advisory boards
- Full-time non-merit employees, excluding maintenance, clerical, secretarial or similar roles
- Chief clerks, chief managers and chief administrators, including their county equivalents
- Any public official or public employee whose primary duty is to invest public funds
- Chief and assistant county building inspectors
- County or municipal administrators with authority to grant or deny land development permits
- Chief municipal clerks, chiefs of police, and fire chiefs
- City and county school superintendents and school board members; school principals and administrators
- Purchasing and procurement agents with authority to make any purchase
- Directors and assistant directors of state agencies
- Chief financial and accounting directors, and chief grant coordinators
- Employees of the Legislature, excluding maintenance, clerical, secretarial or similar positions
- Employees of the judicial branch, including active supernumerary district attorneys and judges, excluding maintenance, clerical, secretarial or similar positions
- All full-time supervisory public employees
