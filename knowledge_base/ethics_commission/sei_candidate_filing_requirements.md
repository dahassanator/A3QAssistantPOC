# Statement of Economic Interests: Requirements for Candidates

## Who must file

Candidates at every level of government (state, county, and municipal) are required to submit a Statement of Economic Interests.

## Deadlines

- Standard (party) candidates: not more than five days after the deadline to file a declaration of candidacy, under Ala. Code § 17-13-5.
- Independent candidates: not more than five calendar days after the date of the first primary election.
- Municipal candidates: not more than five days after the deadline for the municipal candidate to file his or her qualifying papers with the election official.

## Consequence of not filing

A candidate who fails to submit the required statement is deemed not qualified, and the appropriate election official shall remove the name of the candidate from the ballot. Ala. Code § 36-25-15(c).

## If you already filed this year

A person who has already submitted a current Statement of Economic Interests before the declaration deadline does not have to file again. They must document this to the Commission, ideally by giving the confirmation number to the party chair or election official, who transmits it to the Ethics Commission. Ala. Code § 36-25-15(b).

## Note on a conflicting deadline

The Secretary of State's "2026 Independent Candidate Ballot Access" guide states that an independent candidate must file the Statement of Economic Interests not more than 5 days after submitting the ballot access petition. This Ethics Commission document instead ties the independent candidate's deadline to the first primary election. Because the petition itself is due by the date of the primary election, the two usually land close together, but they are not the same rule. The Ethics Commission administers this filing, so confirm with them (334-242-2997 or candidates@ethics.alabama.gov) before relying on either date.
