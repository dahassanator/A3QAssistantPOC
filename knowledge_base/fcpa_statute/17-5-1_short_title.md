# Ala. Code § 17-5-1 - Short Title

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the "Fair Campaign Practices Act."

History: Acts 1988, 1st Ex. Sess., No. 88-873, p. 397, §1; §17-22A-1; amended and renumbered by Act 2006-570, p. 1331, §23.
