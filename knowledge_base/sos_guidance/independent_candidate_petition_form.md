# Alabama Ballot Access Petition for Independent Candidates (sample form)

This is a blank form, not a guidance document. It is included so the assistant can describe what the petition looks like and what each signer must provide.

## Header

OFFICE OF THE ALABAMA SECRETARY OF STATE
ALABAMA BALLOT ACCESS PETITION FOR INDEPENDENT CANDIDATES
PAGE ____ OF ____
STATE OF ALABAMA
Revised 12.2.2019

## Petition statement printed at the top of each page

Pursuant to Section 17-9-3, Code of Alabama, 1975, as amended, we, the undersigned, are qualified electors in ____________ (name of political subdivision for ballot access, such as State of Alabama for statewide ballot access, or county or title of office including district or place number, if applicable) and as such hereby request and petition that the name of ____________ (name of candidate) be certified as an independent candidate for the office of ____________ (title of office, including district or place number, if applicable) and placed on the official ballot in the ____________ (type of election: special or general) election to be held on ____________ (date of election; for a special election, the date of the election is not required.)

## Instructions printed on the form

PLEASE WRITE CLEARLY AND LEGIBLY - PLEASE COMPLETE ALL FIELDS - ILLEGIBLE OR INCOMPLETE ENTRIES MAY NOT BE COUNTED

## Signature table columns

Each numbered row (1 through 10 per page) collects:

| Column | What the signer provides |
|---|---|
| # | Row number |
| Printed Name | First and Last |
| Date of Birth | MM/DD/YYYY |
| Residence Address | Physical address, no P.O. boxes |
| City | |
| Signature | |
