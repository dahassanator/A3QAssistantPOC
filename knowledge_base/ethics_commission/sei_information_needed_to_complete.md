# Information Needed to Complete the Statement of Economic Interests

- Candidate information (if applicable), including office sought and agency type
- Personal contact information, including name, address, email address, and phone number
- Public position information (elected official, appointed official, employee, etc.) and agency name(s)
- Employment information for any non-government work performed during the reporting year
- Spouse's employment information
- Total household income range
- Family member information, including:
  - Spouse's name, address, and employer/business
  - Dependent names, addresses, and employment (if any)
  - Names of living adult children
  - Names of parents (living and deceased)
  - Names of siblings (living and deceased)
  - Names of spouse's living parents
- Income sources for you, your spouse, and dependents, including salaries, fees, dividends, profits, commissions, interest income, and other compensation
- Names of businesses from which income was received and the applicable income range
- Information regarding ownership of 5% or more of any business by you, your spouse, or dependents
- Information regarding service as an officer, director, trustee, or consultant for any business
- Income received from those business positions (if applicable)
- Outstanding debts owed to businesses operating in Alabama as of December 31 of the reporting year
- For public officials, information regarding rent or lease income received from Alabama governmental agencies, including details of any lease or rental agreements
- Certification and signature affirming the accuracy of the information provided

## Where to file

Online: https://seiform.alabama.gov/
Annual deadline: no later than April 30 of each year, for the preceding calendar year.
Questions: sei@ethics.alabama.gov or (334) 242-2997
