# Ala. Code § 17-5-18 - Failure to File Required Statement or Report (REPEALED)

Repealed by Act 2013-311, §3, effective August 1, 2013.

This section has no active statutory text. It formerly addressed failure to file campaign finance documents. Penalties for late or inaccurate filings are now handled under § 17-5-19 (criminal violations) and § 17-5-19.1 (civil penalties).

History: Acts 1988, 1st Ex. Sess., No. 88-873, p. 397, §21; §17-22A-21; amended and renumbered by Act 2006-570, p. 1331, §24.
