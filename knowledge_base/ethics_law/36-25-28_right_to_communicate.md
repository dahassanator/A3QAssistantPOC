# Ala. Code § 36-25-28 - Chapter Not to Deprive Citizens of Constitutional Right to Communicate with Members of Legislature

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as to deprive any citizen, not lobbying, of the citizen's constitutional right to communicate with members of the Legislature.

History: Acts 1973, No. 1056, p. 1699, §25; Acts 1975, No. 130, p. 603, §1; Acts 1995, No. 95-194, p. 269, §1.
