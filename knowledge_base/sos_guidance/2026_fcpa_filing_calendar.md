# 2026 FCPA Filing Calendar (2026 Election Cycle)

## How to read this calendar

FCPA Filing Calendar
2026 Election Cycle
Statewide Primary Election – Tuesday, May 19, 2026
Primary Runoff Election – Tuesday, June 16, 2026
General Election – Tuesday, November 3, 2026
Purple lines indicate deadlines involving monthly reports. Monthly reports are due on the second business day of the subsequent month beginning twelve (12) months before the election date upon crossing the $1000 raising/spending threshold OR upon qualifying with a political party [§17-5-8(b)(1)]. Monthly reports are not due if filing weekly reports [§17-5-8(i)].
Blue lines indicate deadlines involving weekly reports. Beginning four (4) weeks before the election date, weekly reports covering the period Saturday through Friday are due on the Monday of the subsequent week [§17-5-8(b)(2)]. The last weekly report is not due if filing daily reports. [§17-5-8(i)]
Red lines indicate deadlines involving daily reports. Beginning on the 8th day prior to the election date, daily reports are due for candidates and PACs that receive or spend $5,000 or more on that day with a view toward influencing the election. Daily reports are only due on those days that the $5,000 threshold is met. Daily reports only apply to legislative, state school board, and statewide candidates. Circuit, district, county, and municipal candidates are not required to file daily reports [§17-5-8(b)(3)].
Principal campaign committees and PACs must file a report disclosing the receipt of any single contribution of $20,000 or more within two (2) business days of receiving the contribution if the contribution has not already been reported in a scheduled finance disclosure report. [§17-5-8.1(c)]
Green lines indicate the legislative blackout beginning and ending dates. The FCPA prohibits candidates for legislative and statewide offices from raising money while the Alabama Legislature is in session, except within 120 days of a primary, primary runoff, or general election. [§17-5-7(b)(2)]
Electioneering communication is defined as any communication disseminated through any federally regulated broadcast media, any mailing, or the distribution, electronic communication, phone bank, or publication in which the advertisement: (1) contains the name and image of a candidate; (2) is made within 120 days of an election in which the candidate will appear on the ballot; (3) the only reasonable conclusion to be drawn from the presentation and content of the communication is that is intended to influence the outcome of an election; and (4) entails an expenditure in excess of $1,000.00 [§17-5-2(a)(6)]
Party candidates intending to participate in the 2026 primary election may not begin soliciting or accepting contributions until May 19, 2025.
Candidates may loan funds to their campaigns prior to May 19, 2025.
Independent candidates intending to participate in the 2026 general election may not begin soliciting or accepting contributions until Nov. 3, 2025.
Independent candidates may loan funds to their campaigns prior to Nov. 3, 2025.
